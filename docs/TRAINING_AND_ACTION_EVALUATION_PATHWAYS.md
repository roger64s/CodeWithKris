# Training and Action-Based Evaluation: Three-Phase Commercial Pathway

*Current implementation reference for the Universal Foundation, flexible Commercial Task Tracks, and Applied AI & Workflow Execution.*

## Purpose

CodeWithKris helps learners move from communication confidence to practical commercial work and responsible AI-assisted execution. Accessible practice, small real-world tasks, reflection, mentoring, and collaborative delivery shape support and work opportunities. Learners are not ranked, labelled, or automatically selected by software.

## Current Training Journey

| Phase | Training focus | Current activities | Evidence of progress |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Universal Foundation | Baseline professional vocabulary, confidence, digital etiquette, and work readiness | Active listening and de-escalation, professional text and email, and synchronous or asynchronous voice clarity | Conversational onboarding, intent mapping, practice participation, and completed foundation missions |
| 2. Commercial Task Tracks | Flexible specialization in high-demand commercial workflows | Lead Generation, Appointment Fixing, Follow-Up Management, and Customer Service | Micro-task artifacts, real-world scenario responses, AI-assisted reflection, and human feedback |
| 3. Training is self-paced and can be supported by a mentor or learning pod. Accessibility needs and preferred forms of support should be agreed with the learner.

## Practical Trial Flow

Each learner completes the following low-stakes flow:

1. Describe a learning or contribution goal.
2. Record availability, passion areas, and preferred support.
3. Select Lead Generation, Appointment Fixing, Follow-Up Management, or Customer Service.
4. Respond to the selected commercial scenario with a first approach.
5. Ask the platform coaching assistant a useful question, when configured.
6. Reflect on what to keep or change.
7. Use the resulting artifact in a mentor or learning-pod conversation.

The coaching assistant may provide one observation, one next experiment, and one clarifying question. Its response is optional and does not make matching or hiring decisions.

## Phase 1: Universal Foundation

**Focus:** Build baseline professional vocabulary, confidence, digital etiquette, and readiness for work.

**Current modules:**

- Active listening and de-escalation: understand needs, acknowledge concerns, and reduce friction respectfully.
- Professional text and email: write clear, concise, appropriately structured workplace messages.
- Voice clarity: practice synchronous calls and asynchronous voice updates at the learner's own pace.

**Action-based evaluation:** Conversational onboarding and intent mapping capture goals, availability, passion areas, and preferred support. Learners share only what helps the learning pod adapt opportunities and support.

## Phase 2: Commercial Task Tracks

**Focus:** Modular, practical execution of specific commercial workflows. Learners can choose a track that fits their goals and can revisit another track later.

### Lead Generation

Training covers prospecting scripts, trustworthy research, data hygiene, qualifying criteria, and respectful outreach.

**Trial:** A local services campaign needs ten suitable prospects, but the contact list is incomplete. The learner describes the first small action they would take.

### Appointment Fixing

Training covers calendar management, time-zone clarity, overcoming scheduling friction, accessible choices, and confirmations.

**Trial:** A qualified prospect is interested, but two attempts to schedule a call have failed. The learner describes the next message and calendar action.

### Follow-Up Management

Training covers contextual post-check-ins, lead nurturing, useful reminders, next-action planning, and feedback collection.

**Trial:** A prospect attended a product conversation but has not replied to the promised next steps. The learner describes the follow-up they would send.

### Customer Service

Training covers empathy-first troubleshooting, de-escalation, ticket management, responsible ownership, and clear progress messages.

**Trial:** A customer reports a duplicate charge and is frustrated after waiting for a reply. The learner describes the first response and operational step.

**Action-based evaluation:** Each track uses a micro-task and a real-world scenario. The learner records a first approach, asks the platform coaching assistant a useful question when configured, and reflects on what to keep or change.

## Phase 3: Applied AI & Workflow Execution

**Focus:** Use technical tooling and AI assistance to scale productivity while retaining human judgment and accountability.

**Current modules:**

- Draft and revise clear customer, prospect, and internal responses with AI assistance.
- Automate repeatable text tasks and check outputs before use.
- Organize CRM contacts, activities, next steps, and outcomes consistently.
- Execute technical or operational steps, verify results, and explain the work.

**Action-based evaluation:** Formative learning-pod progress emphasizes mentorship engagement, tool utilization, collaboration, iteration, and reflection. The platform coaching assistant offers an observation, a next experiment, and a clarifying question; it does not decide readiness or assignment.

## Formative Progress Evidence

Progress develops through real participation rather than a single result. The platform currently supports these evidence categories:

- Mentorship engagement.
- Tool utilization.
- Collaboration.
- Iteration after feedback or rework.
- Reflection.

Sprint task starts create tool-use evidence. Submissions and approvals create collaboration evidence. Reopened work creates iteration evidence. Mentor task reviews create mentorship evidence. Learning-pod members can review this history within the relevant workspace.

## Readiness and Human Review

A learner becomes eligible for human-reviewed team matching when they have:

- Completed a practical commercial task trial.
- Completed all required training missions.
- Contributed at least one peer review.

Eligibility is a prompt for a person to review the evidence; it is not an automatic assignment. A recorded human reviewer and completed practical trial are required before a candidate can be marked as matched.

## Current Scope and Next Step

The current application implements all three phase headings and mission cards, conversational intent capture, four selectable commercial task trials, optional AI coaching, formative pod evidence, peer work review, and human-reviewed matching controls.

Future curriculum releases can deepen each track with additional introductory, supported-delivery, and client-sprint micro-tasks while retaining learner choice, accessibility adjustments, mentor feedback, and human-reviewed matching.
